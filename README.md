**The meme:** a deadpan gamer slumps on his couch, controller in hand, in front of a neon Vice City sunset — behind him a mountain of torn-off calendar pages from 13 years of waiting. Top caption: "WAITED 13 YEARS FOR GTA 6". Bottom caption: "5 MINUTES INTO THE GAME: 'SO... WHEN IS GTA 7?'" GTA VI launched on 19 November 2026, and this is the launch-week truth nobody wants to admit: the wait became the hobby.

The meme is posted publicly on X from the entrant's own account; once it is live, its URL goes into `post.json` (the `post_url` field) and is handed in with the entry. Running the entrypoint copies `meme.png` to the output directory and writes `post.txt` there with the post's exact caption and, once known, its URL.

**The meme on X:** https://x.com/KuntulZin/status/2123545902187229563

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
